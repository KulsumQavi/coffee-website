BrewHaven is a gathering place shaped by quiet ritual — where the warmth of the room, the pour of a single-origin roast, and the scent of biscuits fresh from the oven ask you to stay a little longer.

Check Menu

Founded in 2025 in the heart of Mayfair

What began as a single counter and a careful pour has become a fixture of the neighbourhood — a hidden address passed on by word of mouth, and a standard of quality we refuse to compromise.

Our team is devoted to a single pursuit — perfecting every cup, and building a culture of care around it.

Stop by, explore an ever-evolving lineup of seasonal roasts and biscuits, and become part of a community built one cup at a time. We'd love to have you.
